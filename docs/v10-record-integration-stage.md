# v10 record integration groundwork

2026-10-02. These internal stages do not enable a playable v10 entry or save migration.

## Maintenance and research

- The original v9 maintenance validator and exact nine-field payment shape remain unchanged
- Shared research/period query signatures now consume only their actual record dependencies;
  original algorithms and version-owned wrappers remain fixed
- The new early maintenance stage validates the complete payment chronology and all L1
  settlements. Library rate tags reject; unrelated tagged alchemy settlements are explicitly
  deferred until genuine upgrade records have been authenticated
- The final rate leaf requires L1 on the exact upgrade-completion tick and L2 afterward,
  with exact upgrade reference and paired L2 material settlement
- Historical L1 work queries retain every clock anchor and never borrow an L2 paid interval

## Lifecycle evidence

- A fixed v10 lifecycle inspector uses the new World identity and unchanged permanent-build
  context, preserving cultivation, estate, archive, event mirror and clock checks
- Its opaque evidence is bound to the exact source World and canonical data, rejecting copied
  tokens, v9 tokens, foreign Worlds and source mutation
- Death facts retain the unavailable expiry event separately from the finalized death event;
  only archived, finalized identities can produce historical terminal-reference bounds
- This completed-record evidence cannot authorize transient pre-retirement cancellation

## Quiet-source diagnostics

- The new diagnostic gate first uses the unchanged complete, fully funded v9 admission
- It reports actual active work, planned construction, automatic scheduling, progression and
  pending lifecycle paths without cancelling or rewriting anything
- Expired maintenance, completed history, training/rest settings and legal pause/speed survive
- Empty diagnostics are not conversion, target-v10 admission or permission to write a save

old maintenance, research and consumer rejection plus the new maintenance, lifecycle and
quiet-source gates passed 223 tests. The separate unchanged clock/care regression passed
